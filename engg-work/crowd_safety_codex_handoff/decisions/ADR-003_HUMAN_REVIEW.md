# ADR-003 — Human-Reviewed Escalation

## Status
Accepted.

## Decision
The system creates evidence-backed safety incidents for a human operator. It does not autonomously dispatch police, fire, or medical services.

## Why
- safety-critical false positives/negatives;
- limited public-data generalisation;
- capstone scope;
- governance/privacy;
- the intended output is decision support, not autonomous enforcement.
